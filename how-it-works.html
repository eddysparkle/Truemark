How it works

  1. Scan or search — barcode, label photo, or product name.
  2. See what we found — public catalogues, TrueMark index, and manufacturer-registered records where available.
  3. Check the pack — compare name, brand, NAFDAC number, and seller trust yourself.
  4. Use official channels — NAPAMS, Greenbook, MAS where applicable.
  5. Report issues — help the community grow safer coverage over time.

TrueMark does not claim every product in the world can be identified. When information is missing, we say so and suggest the next useful step.
