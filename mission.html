Our mission

TrueMark exists so people in Nigeria and beyond can check product information before they buy — using barcodes, labels, and official guidance links — without being misled by empty “100% genuine” claims.

We prioritise transparency: catalogue matches, manufacturer records, and regulatory links are shown with clear limits. A match is not physical authenticity.

Our long-term goal is a trustworthy product intelligence platform that expands coverage honestly as data sources and partnerships allow.
