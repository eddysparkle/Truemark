Privacy Policy

TrueMark (“we”, “the app”)
Last updated: 17 November 2026

TrueMark helps users check product information and find guidance for official regulatory checks (including NAFDAC-related public portals). This policy explains what data the app uses and what it does not do.

1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to the TrueMark website and any TrueMark mobile app wrapper that loads the same service (including versions distributed via app stores).

2. Data we process

On your device

  • Camera / photos: used only when you choose to scan a barcode or photograph a product label. Images are processed to provide recognition or lookup results.
  • Scan history: recent activity may be stored locally on your device (for example in browser storage) so you can see past checks.
  • Search text: product names, barcodes, or registration numbers you enter are sent to our servers or public catalogue services to return results.

On our servers (Cloudflare Worker / related infrastructure)

  • Technical request data needed to run the service (for example timestamps and standard server logs).
  • Product lookup and recognition requests you submit while using the app.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not require an account for basic product checks in the current beta.
  • We do not claim that a catalogue match or NAFDAC number read from a pack proves a physical item is genuine.

4. Third-party services

TrueMark may connect to third-party services to provide features, including:

  • Cloudflare (hosting, API, AI processing infrastructure)
  • Public product catalogues (for example Open Food Facts / related open catalogues)
  • Official regulatory websites such as NAFDAC NAPAMS or Greenbook (opened in your browser)
  • External store search links (for example Google, Jumia, Konga) when you choose “Find sellers”

Those services have their own privacy policies. When you leave TrueMark for an external site, their terms apply.

5. Permissions (mobile)

  • Camera: barcode scan and label photos only, when you start those actions.
  • Internet: required for lookups, recognition, and official/external links.
  • Photos/files (if applicable): only when you pick an image from your device.

6. Children

TrueMark is not directed at children under 13. Do not submit personal information about children through the app.

7. Data retention

Local history stays on your device until you clear site data or app storage. Server logs and operational data are kept only as long as needed to operate and secure the service.

8. Security

We use standard hosting and transport security (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Your choices

  • You can refuse camera access in system settings (scanning features will be limited).
  • You can clear local history by clearing browser/app site data.
  • You can stop using the service at any time.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy: successfulutomi@gmail.com

11. Changes

We may update this policy as TrueMark grows. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
