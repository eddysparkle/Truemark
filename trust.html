Trust & safety

  • Consumers keep essential checks free.
  • Manufacturers cannot pay for a favourable authenticity result.
  • Missing catalogue data does not mean a product is fake.
  • A barcode or name match is not proof the physical item is genuine.
  • Manufacturer-registered products are labelled as such — not as regulator verification.
  • Admin tools are restricted to platform staff.

When in doubt, rely on the physical pack, authorised sellers, and official NAFDAC channels.
